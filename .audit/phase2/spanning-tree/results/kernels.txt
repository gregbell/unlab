A cut is of the cable that carries a stream of pings from node3 to node1, fifty a second. The blocked port carries when its switch says
that the port is its way to the root and forwards, which is a few turns of the fabric after the cut. A ping is answered when the next one
is sent, so the answer after the cut comes one interval of 20 ms after the answer before it, and none is lost: the stream bounds that
number, and the protocol does not.
