TCP between two guests with spanning tree off, on one switch and across one cable between two switches, sides in turn:
ONLY="one,one cable" node .audit/phase2/cables/drive.mjs tcp 6 42a808c=<dist> c9ba895=<dist>
42a808c is main. c9ba895 is this slice. 16 cores, load average 0.9 before the run. Each run sends 256 MiB after 16 MiB to warm up, and
fails on a short transfer. The first run of each also has the cores that the busiest threads used in a second transfer: the page's own
thread is at 0.92 to 0.95 of a core, which is what bounds the rate.
